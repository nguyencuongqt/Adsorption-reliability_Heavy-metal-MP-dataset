# Water Research Submission Checklist

Prepared against the current official author guidance for Water Research and Elsevier policy pages:

- Water Research Guide for Authors: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/water-research/publish/guide-for-authors
- Elsevier AI policy for authors: https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/the-use-of-generative-ai-and-ai-assisted-technologies-in-writing-for-elsevier

## Core manuscript package

- confirm article type and final manuscript title
- keep the abstract within the journal word limit
- keep highlights to 3-5 bullets and no more than 85 characters per bullet
- keep keywords within the journal limit
- insert all in-text citations and the final reference list
- confirm figures, tables, and SI are cited in order in the manuscript

## Journal-specific files

- prepare the graphical abstract for upload
- prepare highlights as a separate file
- prepare supplementary information as a separate file
- export editable figure files and final high-resolution figure files

## Declarations and metadata

- add author names, affiliations, and corresponding author details
- complete CRediT author roles
- add declaration of competing interest
- add funding statement
- confirm data availability statement
- confirm declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in writing

## Final scientific checks

- keep claims descriptive where fold counts are small or variance is high
- keep null-feature benchmarking in the interpretation narrative
- keep `leave_one_study_out` framed as an auxiliary stress test unless moved fully to SI
- confirm figure captions match the corrected `RQ2` story

## Before upload

- open the DOCX and SI one last time and check layout visually
- confirm filenames are stable and submission-system friendly
- prepare a short cover letter aligned to Water Research scope
