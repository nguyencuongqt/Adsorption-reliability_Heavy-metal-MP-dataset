# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, **"Grouped Validation and Null-Feature Audits Reveal Reliability Limits in Literature-Derived Models of Heavy-Metal Adsorption by Microplastics,"** for publication in *Water Research*.

This study addresses a reliability problem that is increasingly important for environmental machine learning on heterogeneous literature-derived datasets. Using a compiled dataset of heavy-metal adsorption by microplastics, we show that random row-level validation can materially overstate predictive performance relative to grouped experiment-level and grouped study-level validation. We also demonstrate that interpretation conclusions depend strongly on the feature-importance method and that synthetic null features provide a practical audit for spurious attribution.

We believe the manuscript fits *Water Research* because it focuses on robust environmental data interpretation, cross-study generalization, and methodological reliability in a topic relevant to contaminant behavior and data-driven water-quality research. The manuscript is not framed as a benchmark contest; instead, it provides guidance on how predictive and interpretive claims should be qualified when literature-derived water-environment datasets are strongly grouped and incomplete.

Potential contributions of the work include:

- a grouped-validation framework for assessing realistic generalization in literature-derived adsorption datasets
- a null-feature audit strategy for interpretation reliability under environmental tabular data
- a conservative discussion of what machine-learning importance can and cannot support mechanistically

This manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to *Water Research*.
